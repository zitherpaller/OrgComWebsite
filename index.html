Discussion of organizational commitment

Affective commitment At CyberKada, this is reflected in the members' genuine interest in cybersecurity, their enthusiasm for participating in organizational activities, and their shared passion for making a positive impact in the digital community. Members remain engaged because they value being part of the organization and believe in its mission.
Continuance commitment Within CyberKada, members recognize the value of the experiences, knowledge, opportunities, relationships, and skills they gain through their involvement. The responsibilities they undertake and the progress they contribute to encourage them to remain committed and continue supporting the organization's initiatives.
Normative commitment At CyberKada, this is demonstrated through members' dedication to fulfilling their assigned roles, supporting fellow members, and contributing to organizational objectives. Members recognize their responsibilities and remain committed to upholding the organization's values, maintaining teamwork, and fulfilling the trust placed in them.

SYSTEMS PERSPECTIVE

CyberKada's officers, committees, and members work as interconnected parts of one organizational system.

Chosen framework & description

SYTEMS PERSPECTIVE

CyberKada adopts the Systems Perspective, viewing the organization as an interconnected system where every officer, committee, and member contributes to achieving shared objectives.

Each position has its own responsibilities, but no individual or committee operates independently. Through effective communication, information-sharing, collaboration, and feedback, all organizational components work together to promote cybersecurity awareness and responsible digital citizenship.

Key aspects of the framework

Systems Thinking Interdependence Feedback Adaptability

The framework emphasizes that organizational success depends on how effectively its members communicate, coordinate, and contribute to the larger system.
